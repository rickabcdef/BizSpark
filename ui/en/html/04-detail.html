Local shop review-reply desk

A tiny service business: you write polite, on-brand replies to customer reviews for 20 small shops, for a flat monthly fee. No shop, no stock, no staff.

Your first 7 steps

  1. Pick one street with 20+ independent shops (cafés, salons, repairs).
  2. Write 10 reply templates: thank-you, late order, bad review, wrong item…
  3. Print 20 letters with one free sample reply for that shop's worst review.
  4. Walk in on a Tuesday morning, leave the letter, don't pitch.
  5. Offer $49/month for up to 15 replies. First month free.
  6. Deliver in 24 h by email — the whole job is 20 minutes.
  7. Ask shop #1 for one introduction after week three.

Three ways people get this wrong

Don't sell "AI writing" — sell "your reviews get answered by tomorrow". Small shop owners buy the outcome.
Don't take more than 25 clients before you have templates. The job stays small only if the pack is done.
Check local rules on soliciting businesses in person; some towns require a simple permit.
