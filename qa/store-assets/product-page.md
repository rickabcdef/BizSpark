## Full description

**You do not need another course. You need one idea small enough to start this week.**

BizSpark asks five questions about your time, your cash, your skills, and the place you live. It answers with three business ideas that fit what you just told it — not a list of 500, not a motivational article. Each idea comes with the real numbers behind it: what it costs to start, how many hours a week it takes, when the money comes back, who your first customer is, and the three mistakes that sink people doing exactly this.

Then it prints. One page, one PDF, on your own disk. Take it to a bank, show it to a partner, or stick it on the wall.

**Five questions. Three ideas. One page.** That is the whole product, and it is finished in about four minutes.

### Why people keep it

- **One-time purchase.** You buy the app once and it is yours. There is no plan to pick, no tier to upgrade, no counter running.
- **No subscription.** Nothing renews, nothing expires, nothing stops working if a payment fails.
- **No auto-renewal.** There is no billing relationship to cancel, because there is no billing relationship.
- **Works offline.** Every core feature — the questions, the recommendations, the library, the PDF export — runs with the network off. Airplane mode changes nothing.
- **Your data stays on this device.** Your answers, your saved ideas, and your exported plans are files in your own application data folder. They are never uploaded, and there is no account to log into.

### What you get, screen by screen

1. **Welcome** — one button, no sign-up wall.
2. **Five questions** — capital, hours per week, skills, location type, and how soon you need income. Tap-to-answer, no typing.
3. **Three matches** — each card shows start-up cost, weekly hours, and payback period at a glance.
4. **Detail** — the seven steps to launch, a cost breakdown that adds up, where your first customers come from, and the three pitfalls to avoid.
5. **Export** — a one-page PDF written to the folder you choose, with no watermark.
6. **Saved** — keep the ideas you want to compare, revisit them later.
7. **Activate** — paste a license code if you have one; three devices per code, released by hand if you change computers.
8. **Library** — 100 curated small-business ideas, browsable and searchable offline.
9. **Settings** — language, theme, export folder, and a plain-language explanation of what is stored locally.

### Built for small

The ideas are chosen to fit real constraints: a few hundred to a couple of thousand to start, evenings and weekends, no premises, no staff, no inventory you cannot carry. Service businesses, online businesses, local B2B, content work, hands-on trades, and agency-style work — all filtered against what you can actually do where you actually live.

### Honest about what it is not

BizSpark is not a business-plan generator for investors, not a market-research database, and not financial advice. It does not promise revenue. It gives you a small, specific, checkable starting point and the arithmetic behind it, so you can decide for yourself.

## Compliance notes for review

- No account, no login screen, no cloud sync. The app never asks for an email address to function.
- The only outbound request is a single HTTPS `GET` for a public static content manifest, with a 3-second timeout and silent failure. No user data is transmitted.
- No third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDK is bundled.
- Pricing is administered by the store listing, not by the app; the app's interface contains no price figure in any state.
- Support contact and privacy policy are reachable outside the app, as required.
